Privacy Policy for Memory Match

Last updated: [DATE]

This privacy policy explains how Memory Match ("the app", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. Memory Match is a memory tile-matching game published on Google Play.

Information we do not collect

Memory Match does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to play. We do not collect your name, email address, or contact details.

Information stored on your device

Your best scores (moves and time, per category and grid size) are saved locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. This information is never transmitted to us or to any server — it stays on your phone and is cleared if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

Memory Match displays ads through Google AdMob. To show ads (including personalizing them, where permitted), AdMob may collect and use information such as:

  • Device identifiers (such as your advertising ID)
  • General device information (device type, operating system version)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, clicked, or dismissed)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. You can learn more about how Google collects and uses this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads Settings

Children's privacy

Memory Match is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we will address it promptly. Advertising shown in the app is served through Google AdMob in accordance with Google's policies for apps of this type.

Permissions

Memory Match requests internet access, which is required to load and display ads and to check for network connectivity. The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in the app or in applicable law. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
